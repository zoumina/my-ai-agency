# End-to-End Execution
Market intelligence selects opportunities. Research gathers public evidence. Verification cross-checks facts. Opportunity scoring ranks fit. Decision-maker discovery finds trustworthy professional contact methods. Company 360 stores research and communication history. Outreach drafts personalized email. Governance evaluates risk and approval. Conversation handles replies. Pricing, proposal and negotiation operate under profitability rules. Agreement creates a project. Website Factory designs, builds, tests and prepares deployment. GitHub workflow protects main. Monitoring checks production health. Reporting measures outcomes. Learning experiments are bounded and audited.
